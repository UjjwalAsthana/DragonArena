# Dragon Battle – Junior Unity Developer Technical Assessment

A 2.5D top-down battle between two dragons: **Ember** (player) vs **Venom** (AI), in a walled stone arena.

- **Unity version:** 2022.3.39f1 (URP 14)
- **Platform:** Windows (build in `Builds/DragonBattle/`)
- **Scene:** `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity`

## Controls (WASD movement)

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `W A S D` | Move (world-relative, the dragon turns towards its movement) |
| `1` | **Fire Breath** – ranged cone of fire, 40 damage over 1 s, 4 s cooldown |
| `2` | **Tail Whip** – close-range 360° tail spin, 22 damage + knockback, 2.5 s cooldown |
| `3` | **Sky Strike** – take off, chase from the air (invulnerable), dive and land with an area slam, 35 damage + knockback, 8 s cooldown |
| `R` | Restart (also available on the Winner screen) |

Ability keys are buffered for 0.3 s, so pressing a key slightly early still fires the moment the dragon is free.

## How the game works

- **Arena:** 22 × 15 m stone floor, four walls with colliders, plus a safety clamp in `Dragon.cs`, so nobody can leave the play area. Corner braziers have flickering point lights; the directional light casts soft shadows; bloom makes fire glow.
- **Camera:** angled overhead (60°), follows the midpoint of both dragons and zooms out when they are far apart. Hits and landings shake it.
- **Combat:** every ability has damage, cooldown, range, its own animation, particle effect and sound. Damage lands with a white flash, scale punch, spark burst, floating damage number, knockback and screen shake.
- **Enemy AI** (`EnemyAI.cs`): state machine **Idle → Chase → Attack**. It picks abilities by distance (tail when close, fire at range, Sky Strike to close the gap or punish close range) and circles the player while abilities are on cooldown. It uses the same `Dragon.TryUse` method as the player, so it obeys the same cooldowns.
- **UI:** health bars for both dragons (top HUD + floating bars), ability icons with radial cooldown and timer, 3-2-1-FIGHT countdown, slow-motion KO, Winner screen naming the victor with a Restart button.

## Project structure

```
Assets/
  Scripts/
    Core/        Ability, Health, Dragon (movement, knockback, 3 abilities)
    Characters/  PlayerController, EnemyAI, DragonVisual (procedural animation)
    Systems/     GameManager, CameraRig, AudioManager, FeedbackManager, DamagePopup, LightFlicker
    UI/          GameUI, WorldHealthBar
  Editor/        BattleAssetGenerator, BattleSceneBuilder  (menu: Dragon Battle > Build Battle Scene)
  Prefabs/ Materials/ Textures/ Audio/ Scenes/
```

The menu item **Dragon Battle → Build Battle Scene** regenerates textures, icons, sounds, materials, prefabs and the whole scene, so the project can be rebuilt from scratch at any time.

## Asset sources

No third-party or paid assets are used. Everything was created for this project:

- Dragons: built from Unity primitives and animated procedurally in code (`DragonVisual.cs`).
- Textures, ability icons: generated by `BattleAssetGenerator.cs`.
- Sound effects and music loop: synthesised by `BattleAssetGenerator.cs` (WAV files in `Assets/Audio`).
- Particles: Unity Particle System with a generated glow texture.

- **Example where the AI got something wrong:** the hit-spark and shockwave prefabs were saved with an empty material, so hits showed magenta squares in play mode. The material assets had been created in the same editor pass and were not imported yet when the prefabs were saved. It was found by looking at a play-mode screenshot and inspecting the prefab, and fixed by refreshing the asset database right after creating the materials. Another miss: the first wing pose looked like flat boards hanging off the body, so I changed the folded pose to sweep back along the body.
